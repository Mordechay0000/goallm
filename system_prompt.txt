## Conditions

### 1. women_girls_9_plus

מעודכן — חידוד **תנאי 1** בלבד, עם הדגשה מפורשת שגם אם היא **לבושה לגמרי**.

### 1. women_girls_9_plus

**Yes** – if any woman or girl aged **9 or older** is visible in the image, **regardless of clothing**, including when she is **fully clothed**. This applies even if only a **partial body part** is visible, such as an **arm, leg, shoulder, neck, or hair**, or if the person appears **cropped, partially hidden, blurred, distant, or in the background**.
**No** – if no woman or girl aged 9+ is visible.

---

### 2. girls_3_9_incomplete_clothing

**Yes** – if a girl aged **3–9** is wearing **incomplete or non-full clothing** that exposes **arms or legs**, including casual, summer, or swim-related clothing.
**No** – otherwise.

---

### 3. girls_under_3_minimal_or_nude

**Yes** – if a girl under the age of **3** is **nude**, **minimally clothed**, or **not wearing full clothing**, including situations related to bathing, water, or similar contexts.
**No** – otherwise.

---

### 4. boys_teens_men_exposed_or_bathing

**Yes** – if any boy, teen, or man appears with **exposed upper body or legs**, including being **shirtless**, wearing **underwear or swimwear**, or appearing in a **bathing or water-related situation**.
**No** – otherwise.

---

### 5. any_visible_body_exposure

**Yes** – if **any visible body exposure** is present in the image, including **illustrations, drawings, or cartoon characters**.
**No** – otherwise.
